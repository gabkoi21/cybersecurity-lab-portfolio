# Lessons Learned

## Investigate Before Classifying

An alert should not be classified solely from the initial alert information. Additional context and evidence should be reviewed first.

## Correlate Multiple Sources

Alert data, SIEM logs, indicators, reputation information, and investigation resources are stronger when considered together.

## Document for the Next Analyst

Clear SOC L1 documentation helps another analyst understand what was investigated, what was found, and why escalation may be required.

## SOC L1 Responsibility

The simulation reinforced the role of the SOC L1 Analyst as the first line of alert triage, investigation, classification, documentation, and escalation.
